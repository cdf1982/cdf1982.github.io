Details
06Good to know

The honest details.

  • Sussurro’s Privacy Policy & Terms are written by a human (me, Cesare, hi!) to be read by other humans — and you should read them.
  • Sussurro relies on OpenAI’s Whisper models, distributed under the MIT license. OpenAI is neither involved nor affiliated with this app.
  • Whisper models can be quite large — download them only when you won’t incur costs from your provider.
  • Transcription speed depends on your device’s performance and the model you choose.
  • Accuracy greatly depends on the source audio quality and the model chosen for transcription.
  • Transcriptions may include errors, including significant mistakes.
  • Sussurro does not separate different speakers and does not format the output text.
